4H Entry Logic — Sweep & Engulf (XAUUSD, dummy numbers)

Wait for the 4H candle to sweep beyond the previous 4H candle's wick, then close back in the direction of daily bias while fully engulfing the previous candle's body. That close is the entry; the swept wick (± 1 pip) is the stop loss. If price sweeps but doesn't fully reclaim past the prior candle's open, there's no valid entry yet — wait.
